introduction

High Field Technical Services LLC delivers a wide range of high-quality technical and maintenance services across the UAE. Backed by a skilled workforce, advanced tools, and a customer-centric approach, the company ensures every project is completed with precision, safety, and efficiency.

Whether the requirement is routine maintenance, renovation, or specialized installation, High Field tailors its services to meet each client's specific needs — on time and within budget.

The company serves commercial, residential, and industrial clients, with particular strength in Annual Maintenance Contracts, renovation and fit-out works, emergency call-outs, and new installations.

our services

High Field Technical Services offers a full suite of building maintenance and technical works, covering ten core service lines for commercial, residential, and industrial clients across the UAE.

Each service is delivered by skilled technicians using advanced tools, with careful coordination to minimize disruption and ensure precision, safety, and efficiency.

01

Painting & Wallpaper Works

Interior and exterior painting, decorative finishes, and wallpaper installation for residential and commercial properties.

02

Electrical Works

Installation, wiring, fittings, and repairs by qualified technicians to UAE safety standards.

03

Air Conditioning & Ventilation

AC installation, servicing, repair, and ventilation system works for homes and commercial spaces.

04

Plumbing & Sanitary Works

Plumbing installation, leak repairs, sanitary fittings, and maintenance.

05

False Ceiling & Light Partitions

06

Plaster Works & Restoration

07

Floor & Wall Tiling Works

Carpentry & Wood Works

Building Cleaning Works

Building Maintenance Works
