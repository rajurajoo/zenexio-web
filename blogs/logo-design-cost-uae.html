Logo Design Cost in the UAE: Real Price Ranges for 2026

August 14, 2026 · 8 min read

Logo design pricing in the UAE ranges from under 500 AED to well over 15,000 AED. Here is a factual breakdown of what drives that range, so you know what to expect.

Quick Answer: Realistic Logo Design Price Ranges in the UAE

Basic logo-only packages in the UAE typically range from 500-2,000 AED, mid-tier packages including strategy and a small identity system range from 2,500-7,000 AED, and full brand identity engagements with strategy, guidelines, and collateral typically run 8,000-25,000+ AED. Price scales primarily with strategic depth, not visual complexity alone.

Why Logo Pricing Varies So Widely

A 500 AED logo and a 15,000 AED logo can both look visually competent — the real difference is almost always in the strategic work behind it, the number of concepts explored, revision rounds included, and whether brand guidelines and a fuller identity system are part of the deliverable.

Coins and financial planning tools representing budgeting

Tier 1 — Basic Logo-Only Packages (500-2,000 AED)

Typically a single designer, minimal or no discovery process, 1-2 concepts, and limited revisions — reasonable for a very early-stage business needing something functional quickly, with the understanding that a proper identity system will likely follow later.

Tier 2 — Strategy-Led Logo With Basic Identity (2,500-7,000 AED)

Includes real discovery and positioning work, multiple genuinely distinct concepts, a defined color palette and typography system, and basic usage guidelines — the realistic entry point for a business wanting a logo built to last several years without needing a redo.

Tier 3 — Full Brand Identity System (8,000-25,000+ AED)

Comprehensive strategy work, full brand guidelines documentation, and a complete collateral system — see our Branding Services in Dubai guide for what a full engagement at this tier typically includes.

What Drives Cost Beyond These Tiers

Whether the engagement includes company profile design, packaging, or bilingual Arabic-English identity work — see our Bilingual Branding Agency Dubai guide for why bilingual work specifically adds real scope and cost.

Red Flags in Very Low-Cost Logo Offers

Extremely cheap logo offers (under 300 AED) often mean no discovery process, template-based or AI-generated output with no genuine originality check, and restricted or unclear file ownership — the low price is rarely "affordable," it is usually a different, lower-value product entirely.
