PWA vs Native App: Which Should a UAE Business Build?

November 22, 2026 · 5 min read

Not every business needs an app in the App Store. A progressive web app (PWA) can deliver an app-like experience straight from the browser at a fraction of the effort. But it is not right for everything. Here is how to decide between a PWA and a native app.

What Is a PWA?

A progressive web app is a website built to behave like an app. Users can add it to their home screen, it launches full-screen, loads quickly and can work with limited or no connection. It is built with web technology and works on both iPhone and Android from a single codebase.

Where PWAs Shine

  • Lower cost and faster launch than separate native apps.
  • No App Store approval needed, and updates go live instantly.
  • Easy to share by link, which suits marketing and QR codes.
  • Ideal for internal tools, customer portals, booking and ordering.

Where Native Apps Win

  • Deep access to device features such as advanced camera, sensors and Bluetooth.
  • Best performance for graphics-heavy or complex apps.
  • Presence in the App Store and Google Play, which some customers trust and search.
  • More reliable push notification and background behaviour on some devices.

See native vs cross-platform development for the wider comparison.

A Sensible Way to Decide

  1. List the three features the app must have on day one.
  2. Check whether any of them need deep device access.
  3. Estimate how many users will come from search or links versus app store browsing.
  4. Decide whether speed to market or store presence matters more.

For many service businesses, a PWA is an excellent first version that proves demand. You can build a native app later with real usage data.
