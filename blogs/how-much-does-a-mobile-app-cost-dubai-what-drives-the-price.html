How Much Does a Mobile App Cost in Dubai? What Really Drives the Price

October 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Ask ten agencies what a mobile app costs and you will get ten very different answers. That is because the price depends on a handful of decisions you can control. Understanding them lets you budget realistically and compare quotes fairly.

The Number of Features and Screens

A simple app with login, a catalogue and contact details is a very different build from one with payments, live tracking, chat and an admin dashboard. Every feature adds design, development and testing time.

One Platform or Two

Building for iOS and Android separately costs more than a single cross-platform codebase. See our comparison of native vs cross-platform development.

Design Complexity

Custom UI/UX, animations and Arabic right-to-left layouts take more effort than standard components. Good design is an investment in whether people keep using the app.

Backend and Integrations

Connecting the app to your CRM, payment gateway, maps, notifications and other systems often costs more than the screens themselves. The backend and admin panel are part of the price, even though users never see them.

Launch and Ongoing Costs

  • App Store and Google Play developer accounts.
  • Hosting and cloud services.
  • Maintenance, security updates and OS compatibility.
  • Improvements based on real user feedback.

Also factor in discoverability — see app store optimization.

How to Get a Fair Quote

Write down your must-have features, ask each agency to break the quote into stages, and clarify who owns the code. A good partner will start with a discovery conversation, not a flat number.
