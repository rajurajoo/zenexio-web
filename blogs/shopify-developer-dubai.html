Shopify Developer in Dubai: What to Look for Before You Hire

August 1, 2026 · 5 min read

Not every developer who can install a Shopify theme is a Shopify developer. Here is what to actually evaluate before hiring one in Dubai.

Installing a Theme Is Not the Same as Custom Development

Shopify's ecosystem makes it deceptively easy to launch a store — pick a theme, add products, connect a payment gateway, done. That accessibility is exactly why the market is full of people calling themselves Shopify developers who have never actually customised a theme beyond changing colours and swapping a logo. A genuine Shopify developer in Dubai should be able to build custom sections, modify theme logic (Liquid, Shopify's templating language), and integrate apps and APIs that a pure "installer" cannot.

For most growing Dubai retailers, a well-chosen and lightly customised theme is genuinely enough at launch. The distinction matters most once a business needs something the theme marketplace does not offer out of the box — a specific checkout flow, a custom product configurator, or an integration with UAE-specific logistics or payment providers.

UAE-Specific Considerations Most Generic Guides Miss

Payment gateway support matters more locally than most Shopify guides acknowledge — confirming smooth integration with UAE-relevant payment providers and cash-on-delivery workflows (still commonly used in the region) should be a specific, named requirement in any Shopify project brief, not an assumption. Local logistics and shipping integration is the second UAE-specific consideration, since many Dubai and wider-UAE stores rely on regional courier and fulfilment services that are not always plug-and-play with default Shopify shipping settings.

VAT compliance for UAE tax requirements is a third area worth confirming explicitly before development starts — pricing display, invoicing, and tax calculation all need to align with UAE regulations, and this is easy to overlook when using a theme built primarily for other markets.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

Ask to see custom Liquid/theme code work, not just examples of stores using off-the-shelf themes with a new logo. Ask specifically how they handle app conflicts, since a store with too many poorly integrated apps is one of the most common causes of slow load times and checkout bugs on Shopify. Ask what post-launch support looks like — Shopify updates its platform continuously, and a store built without a maintenance plan tends to accumulate small breakages over time. And ask whether they have handled UAE-specific payment gateway or logistics integrations before, since that experience is not universal even among skilled Shopify developers.
