Corporate Video Production Services in Dubai: Types and Use Cases

August 12, 2026 · 9 min read

"Corporate video" covers several genuinely different formats. Here is a factual breakdown of the types of corporate video production services in Dubai and when each is worth the investment.

Quick Answer: The Main Types of Corporate Video

Corporate video production services in Dubai typically cover five distinct formats: brand films for general marketing, explainer videos for products or services, client testimonial videos, internal training content, and event or conference coverage — each with genuinely different production requirements and cost structures.

Type 1 — Brand Films

General-purpose videos establishing brand identity and values, typically used across a website homepage, social media, and pitch presentations. These carry the highest production value expectations since they represent the brand broadly, not a specific product or offer.

Type 2 — Explainer Videos

Focused videos breaking down a specific product, service, or process — often the highest-converting video format for a website or landing page since they directly address a viewer's specific question rather than general brand messaging.

Camera and lighting equipment set up for a video shoot

Type 3 — Client Testimonial Videos

Real client interviews carrying significant trust-building weight, particularly for B2B services and high-consideration purchases — genuine testimonials consistently outperform written reviews for conversion impact.

Type 4 — Internal Training and Onboarding Content

Video-based training scales more consistently than repeated live sessions, and is increasingly expected for growing UAE businesses with multiple locations or high staff turnover roles.

Type 5 — Event and Conference Coverage

Documentation of company events, product launches, or conferences, useful both for immediate social content and longer-term brand archive material.

Which Type Is Worth the Investment First

For most Dubai SMEs, an explainer video for the core product or service typically delivers the clearest return, since it directly supports the specific decision a website visitor is already trying to make — brand films and event coverage matter more once that foundational content exists.
