Website Design Cost in Dubai: What Affects the Price in 2026

November 24, 2026 · 5 min read

Ask three agencies what a website costs in Dubai and you may get three very different quotes — from a few hundred dirhams to tens of thousands. The gap is rarely about greed. It is about what is actually included. Here is what really drives website design cost in the UAE, so you can compare quotes properly.

Why There Is No Single Price

A one-page landing page, a ten-page company website, an online store and a booking platform are different products. Each needs different design effort, features and testing. Anyone quoting a flat price without asking what you need is guessing.

At Zenexio, professional business websites start from AED 699, and more complex builds are priced around the scope. The sections below explain why.

1. Number of Pages and Content

More pages mean more design and development time. Also consider who writes the content: copywriting, photography and translation are often separate costs. A site is only as good as its content, so plan for it early.

2. Design: Template or Custom

A template-based design is quicker and more affordable. A fully custom design built around your brand takes longer but helps you stand out. Neither is wrong — it depends on how much your brand identity matters to your customers.

3. Features and Functionality

  • Contact forms, WhatsApp chat and Google Maps usually sit at the simple end.
  • Booking systems, customer portals and online payments add real development work.
  • E-commerce needs product management, checkout, shipping and tax logic.
  • CRM integration and automation connect the site to your sales process.

Read our guide to the website features that generate leads to decide which ones are worth paying for.

4. Arabic and Multilingual Support

Adding Arabic means more than translating text. The layout must flip to right-to-left, fonts must be chosen carefully and every form and button tested. Planning this from day one costs far less than retrofitting later.

5. Platform and Technology

WordPress, a website builder, Shopify or custom code all price differently and suit different goals. Our comparison of website platforms for UAE businesses helps you pick.

6. SEO, Speed and Security

A site that looks good but loads slowly or cannot be found on Google does not earn its cost. Performance work, search-friendly structure and SSL security should be part of the build. See why speed is a design decision.

Costs After Launch

  • Domain and hosting, usually renewed yearly.
  • Maintenance, updates and security monitoring — see what maintenance should include.
  • Ongoing SEO, content and marketing if you want growth, not just a presence.

How to Compare Quotes Fairly

Ask each agency for a written scope listing pages, features, revisions, languages, hosting and who owns the code. If two quotes differ widely, the scope almost always explains why. For a fuller checklist, read what a website development company should deliver.
