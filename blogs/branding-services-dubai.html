Branding Services in Dubai: A Breakdown of What You Can Actually Buy

August 9, 2026 · 8 min read

"Branding services" covers a wide range of individual deliverables. Here is a clear breakdown of what each one actually is, so you know exactly what to ask for.

Branding Services Is an Umbrella Term

"Branding services" covers a genuinely wide range of individual deliverables, and businesses often assume a quote includes more than it actually does. Breaking down what each individual service actually is makes it much easier to scope exactly what you need — and to compare quotes fairly across providers.

Brand Strategy and Positioning

The foundational service: defining who you serve, what makes you different, and how you want to be perceived, before any visual work begins. Skipping this and going straight to logo design is the most common reason a brand identity ends up looking nice but communicating nothing specific.

Logo and Visual Identity Design

The visual system built on top of that strategy — primary and secondary logo marks, color palette, typography, and iconography. See our Brand Visual Identity guide for why this needs to function as a cohesive system, not a collection of separately-chosen assets.

Brand Guidelines Documentation

A formal document specifying exactly how the identity should and should not be used — logo clear space, color codes, typography rules, tone of voice. This is what keeps a brand consistent as more people (internal staff, external vendors, agencies) touch it over time.

Marketing Collateral and Brand Applications

Business cards, letterheads, presentation templates, and social media templates that carry the identity into everyday business use. For many UAE businesses, this specifically includes a company profile document, a commonly-requested deliverable in this market.

Packaging Design

For product businesses, packaging design carries the brand identity through to the physical shelf or delivery experience — see our Packaging Design Agency guide for what this specific service involves.

Rebranding vs. New Brand Identity

A rebrand is a genuinely different service from a new identity build — it requires auditing what equity exists in the current brand and deciding what to keep versus change, rather than starting from a blank page. Confirm which one you actually need before requesting quotes, since the scoping process differs.

How to Scope Branding Services Correctly

List exactly which of the services above you need before requesting quotes, so you are comparing providers on the same scope. See our Design & Website Pricing Guide for realistic market ranges across each individual service.
