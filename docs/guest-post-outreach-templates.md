# Guest Post Outreach — Email Templates

Use these for real, relevant publications only — not paid "guest post service" sites that sell placements (those are link-farms; Google treats undisclosed paid links as manipulation, and they carry near-zero real authority). Good targets: UAE business/tech press (Gulf News, Khaleej Times, Arabian Business, Entrepreneur Middle East), design/marketing trade publications, and industry-specific blogs whose audience genuinely overlaps with Zenexio's clients (real estate, F&B, healthcare, retail in the UAE).

Before sending: find the actual editor or contributor-guidelines contact on the target site itself (usually in the footer or an "editorial guidelines" / "contribute" page) — don't send to a generic info@ address, it rarely reaches anyone who can say yes.

---

## Template 1 — Cold pitch with a specific article idea (best conversion rate)

**Subject:** Article idea for [Publication Name]: [specific topic]

Hi [Editor's name],

I'm [your name] from Zenexio, a creative agency based in Dubai working across web development, branding, and digital marketing for clients in the UAE and India.

I read your recent piece on [specific article they published — shows you actually read the site] and wanted to pitch a follow-up angle your readers might find useful: **[specific, narrow article title — e.g. "Why WhatsApp Commerce Is Outperforming Traditional E-Commerce for UAE Retailers"]**.

The piece would cover:
- [specific point 1, ideally with a real number or example]
- [specific point 2]
- [specific point 3]

I can have a draft (roughly [800-1200] words) to you within [X] days, written specifically for [Publication Name]'s audience rather than repurposed from elsewhere. Happy to adjust the angle if something else would serve your readers better.

Let me know if this is of interest.

Best,
[Your name]
Zenexio — zenexio.pro

## Notes on doing this well

- **One pitch per email, one publication at a time.** Mass-blasting the same pitch to twenty editors reads as spam and burns the relationship before it starts.
- **The article has to actually be good and not a thinly-veiled ad.** One contextual link back to zenexio.pro (or a specific relevant page, like a blog post) in the author bio or body is normal; multiple self-promotional links will get the piece rejected or the link stripped.
- **Track outreach in a simple log**: publication, contact, date sent, response, published URL — so you know what worked and can follow up once (not repeatedly) if you don't hear back after 1-2 weeks.
- **Realistic expectations**: a genuine guest post placement on a real UAE publication typically takes days-to-weeks of back-and-forth (pitch → approval → draft → edits → publish), not an instant listing like a directory. It's slower than directory submissions but the resulting backlink carries real authority.
